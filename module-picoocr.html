Module: picoocr

PicoOCR - text recognition in pure JavaScript. A page image's text lines are found (lib/layout.js) and each is read by a small neural network (lib/model.js) whose weights are part of the code (lib/models/recognizer.js) - no network requests, no files, no dependencies.

(static, constant) MAX_PAGE_SIDE

The longest side, in pixels, a page is analyzed at - larger pages are scaled down first. At this size a phone photo of a letter-size page puts body text near the recognizer's scale.

(static, constant) ORIENTATIONS

The page orientations recognize detects - an orientation's index is its flips: 1 horizontal (mirrored), 2 vertical (both - rotated 180 degrees)
